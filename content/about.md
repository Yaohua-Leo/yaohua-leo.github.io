I am **Yaohua (Leo)**, a mathematics PhD researcher in algebra. My main interests are Jordan algebras, Quillen (co)homology, formalized mathematics, and AI-assisted mathematical research.

I use this site to publish selected research notes, project overviews, expository writing, and reading trails. It is a curated public notebook: polished explanations and useful work in progress may appear here, while private and preliminary material remains outside the site.

## Current focus

- Developing an intrinsic and computable perspective on Quillen (co)homology for Jordan algebras.
- Formalizing reusable Jordan algebra identities in mathlib.
- Designing AI-assisted workflows for mathematical exploration, checking, and exposition.

You can find my public code and projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/Yaohua-Leo).
