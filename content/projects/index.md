These projects connect my research in Jordan algebra with formalization, computation, and AI-assisted mathematical work.

## [[projects/jordan-quillen|Jordan–Quillen]]

An experimental program for developing and computing intrinsic Quillen (co)homology of Jordan algebras.

**Focus:** definitions, cotangent constructions, resolutions, and exact small examples.

## [[projects/mathlib-jordan|Jordan algebra in mathlib]]

A formalization project aimed at a clear, reusable API for Jordan algebra identities and their linearizations.

**Focus:** proof design, reusable lemmas, and compatibility with the wider mathlib ecosystem.

## [[projects/ai4math|AI4Math research workflow]]

A workflow project investigating how AI can help with mathematical research while preserving traceability and independent verification.

**Focus:** exploration, proof auditing, formal checks, and research exposition.

Public code and open project work can be found on [GitHub](https://github.com/Yaohua-Leo).
