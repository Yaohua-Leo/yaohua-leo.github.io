This project develops reusable formal infrastructure for Jordan algebras in Lean and mathlib.

## Objectives

- State Jordan identities in forms that compose well with existing algebraic APIs.
- Formalize linearization arguments with explicit assumptions on scalars and characteristic.
- Build small, well-named lemmas that support later structure and representation theory.
- Keep informal proofs, Lean statements, and machine-checked proofs aligned.

Formalization is valuable here not only as verification. It also reveals hidden assumptions and encourages definitions that can support a larger body of work.
