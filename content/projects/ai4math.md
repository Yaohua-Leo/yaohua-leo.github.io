This project investigates how AI can support serious mathematical work without replacing mathematical judgment.

## Workflow

1. State the problem, assumptions, and acceptance criteria precisely.
2. Use AI to explore definitions, examples, proof strategies, and possible counterexamples.
3. Record dependencies and distinguish proved steps from conjectural ones.
4. Check critical claims independently through proof, exact computation, or formalization.
5. Turn the verified result into a readable research artifact.

The central design principle is **traceability**: a useful system should make it easy to see what was assumed, what was computed, what was proved, and what remains open.
