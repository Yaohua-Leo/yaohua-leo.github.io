This project studies how Quillen homology and cohomology can be formulated and computed intrinsically for Jordan algebras.

## Guiding questions

- What are the appropriate modules, derivations, and universal differentials for Jordan algebras?
- Which model of the cotangent complex is most useful for explicit computation?
- How can cofibrant resolutions be constructed and checked in small examples?
- Which calculations remain stable when the chosen presentation or truncation changes?

## Working method

The project moves between abstract definitions and exact finite calculations. Small examples are used to expose missing hypotheses, test candidate complexes, and separate genuine homology classes from artifacts of a presentation or cutoff.

The goal is a framework whose definitions are conceptually sound and whose computations are independently checkable.
