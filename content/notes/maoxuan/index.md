This collection organizes Chinese-language notes created while reading works associated with Mao Zedong and modern Chinese history.

The collection has two layers:

- **Concept notes** connect people, events, organizations, and political terms.
- **Source-text references** support lookup and should be treated as archival material rather than original writing.

The archive is designed for topic-based search and linked-note navigation. Its presence here does not imply endorsement of the views represented in the source material.
