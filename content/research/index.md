My research is centered on **Jordan algebras**, with a particular interest in structural, homological, and computational questions. I also work on formalization and on methods for using AI carefully in mathematical research.

## Jordan algebras

Jordan algebras sit at an intersection of nonassociative algebra, representation theory, and geometry. I am interested in identities and structure theory, as well as tools that make small examples and general constructions easier to study.

## Quillen (co)homology

My current homological direction asks how Quillen's framework can be developed intrinsically for Jordan algebras. The central objects include derivations, modules, cotangent constructions, cofibrant resolutions, and computations in small examples.

See the [[projects/jordan-quillen|Jordan–Quillen project]].

## Formalization and AI-assisted mathematics

Formalization in mathlib offers a way to turn algebraic arguments into reusable, machine-checked infrastructure. In parallel, I explore how AI can support conjecture formation, proof analysis, computation, and exposition without hiding mathematical gaps.

See [[projects/mathlib-jordan|Jordan algebra in mathlib]] and the [[projects/ai4math|AI4Math workflow]].
